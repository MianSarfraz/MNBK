# Kiln app roadmap

- [x] Phase 1: patheer weekly register, kharcha, ledger, rates, seasons, and qualities.
- [ ] Phase 2: expense khatta, employee and owner ledgers, cash reconciliation, weekly rehra loads, weekly nakasi output, and raw/finished stock reconciliation.
- [ ] Phase 3: customers, multi-quality sales invoices and payments, customer balances, stock warnings, and printable invoices.
- [ ] Phase 4: season and party reports, PDF/Excel exports, full export, and access/polish review. Lovable Cloud already provides daily database snapshots; verify backup guidance rather than duplicating it in-app.

Decisions: Rehra entries are weekly totals by quality and optional source patheer; nakasi entries are weekly totals by quality.
